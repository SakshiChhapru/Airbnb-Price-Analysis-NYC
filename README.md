# 🏠 Airbnb Price Analysis in NYC

Data-driven analysis of pricing drivers in NYC Airbnb market

## 📌 Objective
To analyse whether weather-related variables influence Airbnb pricing in New York City and assess their impact on short-term rental market behaviour.

---

## 🏢 Business Context
Airbnb hosts and property managers aim to optimise pricing strategies based on demand patterns. Understanding whether external factors such as weather influence pricing can help improve revenue strategies and forecasting.

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- Python  
- Pandas, NumPy  
- Matplotlib, Seaborn  
- Jupyter Notebook  

---

## 📂 Dataset
- NYC Airbnb Listings Dataset (Kaggle)  
- NYC Weather Dataset (Temperature data)  

---

## 🔍 Key Analysis Performed
- Data cleaning and preprocessing  
- Merging Airbnb and weather datasets  
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)  
- Correlation and relationship analysis  
- Investigation of pricing drivers  

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- Weather variables such as temperature show minimal direct correlation with Airbnb prices  
- Pricing is significantly influenced by location factors such as neighbourhood and proximity to key areas  
- Room type and property characteristics play a stronger role in determining price than external environmental factors  
- Seasonal trends may impact overall demand, but short-term weather fluctuations do not significantly affect pricing  
- The Airbnb market in NYC is driven more by structural and market variables than by external conditions like weather   

---

## 🚀 Recommendations
- Airbnb hosts should prioritise pricing strategies based on location and property characteristics  
- Seasonal demand patterns should be leveraged for pricing optimisation  
- Weather data can be used for demand forecasting rather than direct pricing decisions  

---

## 💼 Business Impact
This project demonstrates that pricing in the Airbnb market is primarily driven by structural and market factors rather than external environmental conditions. These insights can help hosts and analysts focus on the most impactful variables when optimising pricing strategies.

## 🧠 Final Conclusion

The analysis suggests that weather variables alone are not strong predictors of Airbnb pricing in New York City. 

Instead, pricing is primarily influenced by structural factors such as location, property type, and availability. While weather may impact demand patterns indirectly, it does not significantly determine listing prices.

This highlights the importance of focusing on core market variables when developing pricing strategies.
